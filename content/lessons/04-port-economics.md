# The bank is the only market

You can't trade with the other player in this game, so every conversion goes through the bank:

- **4:1** with no port: give four of one resource, take one of another.
- **3:1** with a generic port: any resource, three at a time.
- **2:1** with a matching port: two of *that* resource for one of any other.

There are nine ports: four generic 3:1 ports and one 2:1 port for each resource. You own a port when you have a settlement or city on one of the two coastal corners it touches, and the best ratio you own for the resource you give is the one that applies.

Every bank trade burns cards: at 4:1 you spend four to gain one. With no other trading in the game, a port is the difference between a hand you can rebuild into what you need and a hand you can't.

---
# What a trade really costs

Count the cards you can't use. Turning four spare sheep into the one ore you need for a city costs three cards net. That's fine if it completes the build *this turn*, and wasteful if it only moves you closer.

Rules of thumb:

- Trade to **finish** something now, not to prepare something later. Tempo beats tidiness.
- A 2:1 port on a resource you produce a lot is usually worth the corner: every second card becomes any card.
- A 3:1 port helps whichever resource ends up in surplus; it rewards flexibility more than one big number does.
- Keep the hand limit in mind. Waiting to pile up four of something while you hold ten cards invites a 7 to take half.

# One resource, one port

A well-known plan: build around one or two strong hexes of a single resource, take the matching 2:1 port, and convert every two spare cards into whatever you're missing. It turns raw volume into flexibility without relying on the other numbers coming up.

It has costs. The robber can sit on your one big hex, and a 7 hits a large hand hard. Your whole engine also rides on a few numbers.

When you read a position, count what each side can *convert*, not just what each side produces. A player with fewer pips but a 2:1 port can turn a surplus into builds faster than a player with more pips and no port.
